# M1.1 review: demo separation (2026-10-23)

Reviewed the uncommitted change on `m1.1-demo-separation` (`css/app.css`, `js/api.js`, `js/app.js`,
`js/demo-backend.js`, `tests/e2e-harden.js`, plus HANDOFF/PLAN/PRIVACY edits) against CLAUDE.md,
PLAN.md M1.1 (SEP-1..SEP-6) and `docs/reviews/2026-10-23-privacy-security.md` (F-01, F-07, F-11).

Build: `node scripts/build-site.mjs dist --demo` (app version 0071bd1f95). `node tests/e2e-demo.js`:
SEP-2, SEP-2 stale, SEP-3, SEP-4, SEP-5 all pass. Screenshots (Playwright 1.56.1, Chromium) in
`/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude-crew-clock/d15b1e1e-b757-5614-a74d-565a7b31f10e/scratchpad/shots/`
(sign-in, location prime, crew home, punch pick, punch done, boss map, boss today; 390x844 and
1280x800, light and dark; plus 360x740 and 320x740 banner crops). Map tiles are blocked in the
sandbox; the console errors (`net::ERR_FAILED`, 6 per phone run, 18 per laptop run) are the stubbed
tile requests, not app errors.

## Verdicts

- **ux-reviewer (showcase): Needs changes.** The banner is on every screen, readable in both
  themes, never overlaps, no horizontal scroll down to 320 px. But it does not say the crew is made
  up (SEP-3's own words), and the bare "chaseholroyd.com" doesn't say it's the way back. Laptop
  visitors also get an AI button that downloads from a CDN.
  - Recruiter, 390x844, 90 s: sees "Sample data" right away; may not get "only in this browser",
    and may not see that the link goes back to Chase's portfolio.
  - Hiring manager, 1280x800, 5 min: the same banner. On Today, "Write it up with AI on this
    phone" shows on a laptop and starts a third-party download (PSR-1).
- **privacy-security: Needs changes (2 x P1).** F-01's remaining Nominatim call is gone (geocode
  stub). F-07's pins are rounded to 3 decimals. `?org=` and the stale session are both handled.
  Two gaps remain. The demo can still load a third-party script and model: user-initiated, but it
  breaks SEP-2, the CLAUDE.md "$0 / no third-party scripts at runtime" rule, and PRIVACY S4's
  claim. And `forgetCrewApp` keys off "no backend configured", not off "this is the demo".
  F-11 is still open.

## Checked and fine

- Banner on every screen walked: sign-in, location prime, home, punch pick, punch done, boss map,
  boss today, at 390x844 and 1280x800, light and dark. No overlap with the "Back" button, the boss
  header or the tab bar. The boss tab bar still fits in 844 px (UX-5). No horizontal scroll at 320,
  360, 390 or 1280.
- Address search: the demo answers from `SAMPLE_PLACES` and no request leaves the origin (SEP-2
  e2e passes with the geocode step).
- `?org=ANY` and a stale `cc.sbSession` keep the demo in demo mode and show the sample crew (SEP-2).
  `cc.orgCode` and `cc.sbSession` are removed.
- `forgetCrewApp` never runs on a correctly configured crew app: `api.js` returns early when
  `backend === 'supabase' && supabaseUrl`. The only gap is the misconfigured case in PSR-2.
- Crew app (`backend: 'supabase'`) shows no banner, portfolio link, demo hints or "pretend" toggle
  (SEP-5 e2e passes).
- Not verified: `https://chaseholroyd.com/` resolves. The sandbox proxy refuses the host. SEP-4 says
  "resolves (not a 404)", so check it once by hand or in CI.
