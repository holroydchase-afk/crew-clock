# Contrast (topic: contrast), from WCAG 2.2

## CONTRAST-1 Text contrast 4.5:1, large text 3:1
- strength: hard
- status: fetched
- source: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum
- tags: contrast, text, wcag, 1.4.3, dark, light
Success criterion 1.4.3 (AA): text and images of text need a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against their background, or 3:1 for large-scale text. Applies in every theme, so measure in dark mode on the real surface color, including secondary and placeholder text. Inactive controls and pure decoration are exempt.

## CONTRAST-2 UI parts and graphics 3:1
- strength: hard
- status: fetched
- source: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#non-text-contrast
- tags: contrast, non-text, borders, icons, focus, wcag, 1.4.11, dark
Success criterion 1.4.11 (AA): the visual information needed to identify a control and its states (input borders, checkbox edges, focus rings) and the parts of graphics needed to understand them need at least 3:1 against adjacent colors. Faint dark-grey borders on a dark surface are the usual failure.

## CONTRAST-3 Never color alone
- strength: hard
- status: fetched
- source: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#use-of-color
- tags: contrast, color, status, wcag, 1.4.1
Success criterion 1.4.1 (A): color is never the only way to convey information, show an action, prompt a response or tell elements apart. Pair status colors with text, an icon or a shape, and check dark mode too, where tints often collapse together.
